How we work

A Florida company, and artwork rights matter

Zebra LLC is a limited liability company registered in the State of Florida, with registration details on public record with the Florida Division of Corporations.

We print what customers supply, and the rights to that artwork are the customer's responsibility — logos, photographs and typefaces included. We also won't fit signage that needs a permit, an electrical connection or a structural fixing without the appropriate licensed trade involved. And colour varies slightly between materials and runs; we'll show you a proof rather than promise an exact match.

Frequently asked

The questions we get most

Can you fix my artwork?

We'll check it free and tell you what's wrong. Fixing or redrawing is quoted separately, and sometimes the honest answer is that the original file is needed.

Will the colour match exactly?

Close, not identical. Colour varies between materials, inks and runs — which is why we proof rather than promise.

Do you install signage?

Yes, and anything requiring a permit, electrical connection or structural fixing is handled with the appropriate licensed trade.

How fast can you turn a job around?

It depends on the item and the finishing. We'll give you a realistic date at quotation rather than an optimistic one.

Can you print someone else's logo?

Only if you hold the rights to use it. That responsibility sits with the customer and we'll ask the question if something looks unclear.

Do you keep artwork for reorders?

Yes, on an account, so a repeat order doesn't start from scratch every time.

Got a job to price?

Send the artwork or the brief. A free artwork check and a written quote within a day.
