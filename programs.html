Large format

Large format punishes low-resolution artwork, so we check files properly and tell you when something won't scale. Materials are chosen for where the piece will live — indoors, outdoors, temporary or for a season.

Program 03 · On the building, on the van

Signage & vehicle graphics

Signage and vehicle graphics are measured on site or from the vehicle rather than from a guess. Anything requiring a permit, an electrical connection or a structural fixing is handled with the appropriate licensed trade, and we'll tell you when that applies.
